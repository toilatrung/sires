\section{Method}\label{sec:method}
Each compilation receives an isolated workspace and a fixed toolchain.
For observations $x_i$, the arithmetic mean is
\begin{equation}\bar{x}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i.\end{equation}
The experiment compares identical inputs under two container runtimes.
